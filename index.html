01 / Design philosophy

Everything belongs to the same family.

No disconnected accents. No random shapes. ChloroPhilix creates distinction through tonal depth, proportion, spacing and a shared geometry.

01 — COLOR

One chromatic voice

A complete violet-indigo scale carries the interface from quiet surfaces to expressive actions.

02 — SHAPE

One geometric rhythm

Soft-cut corners, capsule controls and measured radii make every element feel related.

03 — DEPTH

One sense of depth

Fine borders, controlled shadows and layered surfaces create hierarchy without visual noise.

02 / Chromatic system

Violet, in every dimension.

The family moves from near-white lavender to deep ink-violet. Neutral surfaces give it space to breathe.

04 / Built to customize

Your product. Your expression.

ChloroPhilix is a starting language, not a locked aesthetic. Override tokens globally or scope them to a component tree.

:root {
  --clover-primary: #6252dc;
  --clover-primary-hover: #5142bd;
  --clover-accent: #ebe8ff;
  --clover-radius-lg: 1.4rem;
  --clover-content-width: 84rem;
}
